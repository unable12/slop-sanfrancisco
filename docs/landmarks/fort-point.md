# Fort Point

## Facts
- Coordinates 37 48 38 N, 122 28 38 W (Wikipedia); OSM relation 5504536 at 37.810548,-122.477104: building=fort, building:levels=3, building:material=brick, height 15 m, start_date 1861, ele 3.
- Plan: "modified rectangle with a center parade and two bastions", one entrance on the land front (US National Archives Prologue magazine).
- Tiers: brick and granite fort rising in four tiers; three casemated tiers plus the top (barbette) tier (Prologue). NPS: 30 casemates on each of three tiers; casemate walls five feet thick (NPS tour page); Wikipedia: seven-foot-thick walls.
- Height: UNKNOWN in text. OSM has 3 levels and 15 m.
- Material: brick and granite; started 1853, completed about 1861; cliff cut down from 90 ft (27 m) to 15 ft (4.6 m) above sea level (Wikipedia).
- Golden Gate Bridge south tower and arch pass over it (320 ft arch over Fort Point, Wikipedia).

- OSM footprint (relation 5504536) minimum bounding rectangle: 91.8 x 60.0 m (301 x 197 ft), rotated about 22 degrees.
