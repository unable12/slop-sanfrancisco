# Conservatory of Flowers

## Facts
- Golden Gate Park; oldest building in the park, completed 1879 (Wikipedia; other sources say 1878). Coordinates 37 46 19.2 N, 122 27 36 W (Wikipedia). OSM way 30675038 at 37.772615,-122.460224: building=conservatory, height 15 m, ele 77.
- Overall length: 240 ft (73 m). Central dome rises nearly 60 ft (18 m) high (Wikipedia; the central pavilion is 60 ft high).
- Plan: shallow E-shaped plan on an east-west axis; octagonal central pavilion with arched roof, clerestory and dome; arch-shaped wings; one-story glassed-in gabled vestibule at the entrance; raised masonry foundation.
- Material: wood structural skeleton (arched wood ribs, wood-clad cast-iron columns inside the rotunda) with lapped glass glazing (Wikipedia). Colour: described as "white-washed" (Untapped Cities); repainting is recorded in 1887-90, 1899 and 1946.
- Dome fire note: a search summary says the dome was replaced after a fire in 1883; the Wikipedia text does not say whether the 60 ft dome is original or rebuilt.

- OSM footprint (way 30675038) minimum bounding rectangle: 75.0 x 35.2 m (246 x 116 ft), consistent with Wikipedia's 240 ft length.
