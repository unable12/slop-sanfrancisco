# Alcatraz

## Facts
- Island size: 1,675 ft (511 m) by 590 ft (180 m), 135 ft (41 m) at its highest point at mean tide (Wikipedia, quoting a 1971 documentary). Area 22 acres (8.9 ha) in the infobox (the same infobox also lists 47 acres NRIS, presumably including submerged land). About 1.25 mi offshore.
- Coordinates (island): 37 49 36 N, 122 25 22 W (Wikipedia); OSM relation 20197830 (ele=37).
- Cell house (main prison): built 1909-1912, concrete, designed by Major Reuben Turner; 3-storey cellhouse with four cell blocks (search summary). Length/width not found in text. OSM way 128245373 "Main Prison" at 37.826631,-122.422931 (no height tag).
- Lighthouse: tower 84 ft (26 m) (Wikipedia, Parks Conservancy), tapered octagonal tower with balcony and lantern, reinforced concrete, built 1909; first light 1854. NPS water-tower page implies the lighthouse is 95 ft ("the water tower comes in one foot short at 94 ft"): sources disagree (84 vs 95). Lighthouse coordinates 37 49 34.5 N, 122 25 19.8 W (37.82625, -122.422167). OSM way 99202294: height 26 m.
- Water tower: 94 ft tall, built 1940 by the Bureau of Prisons (NPS). OSM way 660870452 at 37.827573,-122.423932 (no height).
- Other OSM buildings: New Industries Building 2 levels, 9 m (way 24433395); Dining Hall, Building 64, Warden's House (ruin), Powerhouse, Guard Tower (way 1056707180).

- OSM footprints: Main Prison way 128245373 minimum bounding rectangle 65.1 x 49.1 m (214 x 161 ft); Water Tower way 660870452 about 13.4 x 13.3 m (44 ft square). No cellhouse length in text sources.
