# Ghirardelli Square

## Facts
- Domenico Ghirardelli bought the whole block in 1893; former Pioneer Woolen Mills (from 1864) and Ghirardelli chocolate factory; converted by Lawrence Halprin and Wurster, Bernardi & Emmons, opened 1964; NRHP 1982. OSM way 27104863 at 37.805871,-122.422949.
- Rooftop sign: spells GHIRARDELLI; 19 ft (5.8 m) tall x 152 ft (46 m) wide (Color Kinetics, Funcheap), erected 1915, restored 1964, LED refit 2020. Sartle gives a different figure: 25 ft x 165 ft. Letter height alone: UNKNOWN (one search summary says 15 ft letters, unverified). Sign was turned to face the waterfront (Wikipedia).
- Clock Tower: the Clock Tower building exists (lower floors are the chocolate shop; part of it is the Fairmont Heritage Place hotel with 4 floors of rooms). Tower height UNKNOWN.
- Material: the Wikipedia text says "historic brick structures". Brick is therefore the main material. Roof pitch and heights UNKNOWN.

- OSM buildings (heights in m): Cocoa Building way 288388853 (5 levels, 22 m); Chocolate Building way 939539794 (5 levels, 22 m); Clock Tower Building way 939539785 (3 levels, 10 m, at 37.805685,-122.422328); Mustard Building way 939539784 (3 levels, 10 m); Woolen Mill Building way 939539795 (3 levels, 10 m); other blocks around the square 7-15 m. The clock tower's own height is not tagged.

- OSM footprint of the square (way 27104863): minimum bounding rectangle 126.8 x 84.3 m (416 x 277 ft), about 10,700 m2.
