# Painted Ladies (Postcard Row)

## Facts
- Address range 710-720 Steiner Street, facing Alamo Square park. Also called "Postcard Row" and the "Seven Sisters".
- Number of houses: the "Seven Sisters" nickname implies seven; Wikipedia does not state the count explicitly. The row is one of the "best-known" groups of painted ladies.
- Built 1892-1896 by developer Matthew Kavanaugh, who lived next door at 722 Steiner (1892 mansion).
- Style: Victorian houses (Wikipedia gives no sub-style for this row), repainted in three or more colours from the 1960s. Term "painted ladies" popularised by the 1978 book by Baer, Pomada and Larsen.
- OSM: node 822484002 "Painted Ladies" (tourism=attraction) at 37.776224,-122.432786. The row on the west side of Steiner St (lon about -122.4327 to -122.4328, lat 37.7760 to 37.7765) is mapped as seven separate building ways with height and colour, which agrees with the "Seven Sisters" name: 261412896 (37.77607, height 12, #c9b085, gabled roof 3 m), 261412895 (37.776133, 12, #cad8d5, gabled), 261412900 (37.776198, 12, #dadbde, gabled), 261412894 (37.776261, 12, #e4c78b, gabled), 261412879 (37.776323, 12, #d4ceb0, gabled), 261412899 (37.776383, 12, #d5c09d, gabled), 261412887 (37.776469, height 13, #96a55b, hipped roof 4 m). The corner house to the south, way 261412902 (37.775872,-122.432704), is 24 m tall in OSM. Building depth/width not extracted.
