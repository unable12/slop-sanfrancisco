# Sutro Tower

## Facts
- Coordinates 37.75523 N, 122.45284 W (Wikipedia); OSM relation 3829019 at 37.755241,-122.452903.
- Height: 977 ft (297.8 m) above ground (Wikipedia; sutrotower.com). OSM height=298.
- Base ground elevation: 834 ft (254 m) above sea level; tip 1,811 ft (552 m) above sea level (sutrotower.com). Wikipedia infobox altitude 254.2 m; OSM ele=254.
- Structure: three legs (triangle plan), steel lattice/truss. Base triangle legs spaced 45.7 m (150 ft) on a side; waist triangle legs 18.3 m (60 ft) on a side; antenna triangle 30.5 m (100 ft) on a side (Wikipedia infobox). Observation (platform) level 6 at 228.8 m (751 ft) above base; Wikipedia also gives a platform at 232 m (761 ft).
- Crossarms: number and heights UNKNOWN in text sources. OSM (building:part, steel, 3 legs plus bracing parts) has leg segments in 26 m bands with alternating colours #e9e9df (white) and #bf4e3b (red-orange): 0-26 white, 26-52 white (separate part set), 52-78 red, 78-104 white, 104-130 red, 130-156 white, 156-182 red, then red blocks 208-232.25, and an antenna part 232.25-298. Treat this as a rough band pattern only; the OSM parts are not tagged as crossarms.
- Colour: white and "aviation orange" (sutrotower.com FAQ and SF Standard). Wikipedia/KQED mention red-and-white aviation striping; a news item says the 2023-2028 repaint will use "Aviation Red and Aviation White" (search snippet). OSM uses #bf4e3b and #e9e9df.
- Built 1971-1973 (first transmissions July 4, 1973); about 3.5 million lb of steel (1,700 tons) and 15 million lb of concrete in the legs.
- Base footprint: three legs at about 45.7 m triangle side; the concrete pads (OSM, height 12-18 m, buildings on site) are not part of the tower.
