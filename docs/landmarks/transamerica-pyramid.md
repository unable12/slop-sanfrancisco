# Transamerica Pyramid

## Facts
- Address 600 Montgomery St; coordinates 37.7952 N, 122.4028 W (Wikipedia). OSM way 24222973 at 37.795166,-122.402786.
- Height: 853 ft (260 m) is the single height Wikipedia gives (structural height, roof/tip); OSM height=260. Top occupied floor (floor 48): 695 ft (212 m). The spire is stated as the top 212 ft (65 m), which does not match 853 - 695 = 158 ft; the sources are inconsistent, so a modeller should pick one (spire base at about 641 ft per the 212 ft figure, or at about 695 ft per the top-floor figure).
- Floors: 48 (Wikipedia; one other source says 49 stories).
- Base width: 175 ft (53 m) (vibrationdata.com). OSM footprint minimum rectangle: 54.5 x 54.3 m (179 x 178 ft), bearing about 9 degrees from north-up, i.e. a square of about 54 m.
- Width at top floor: UNKNOWN (a search snippet said the 48th floor is 45 ft per side and the 5th floor, the largest, is 145 ft; the page was not opened, so unverified).
- Wings: two triangular buttresses ("wings") start at the 29th floor and rise to the top; east wing holds the elevator shaft, west wing holds a stairwell and a smoke tower (Wikipedia; vibrationdata.com; PCAD).
- Spire: top 212 ft (65 m), aluminium-panel clad; four cameras at its top; "Crown Jewel" beacon lit on holidays.
- Facade: precast concrete panels covered in white crushed-quartz aggregate (PCAD), top clad in aluminium panels.
- Base: the Wikipedia text mentions a four-storey base section with a 9 ft (2.7 m) thick foundation but does not describe columns or trusses; vibrationdata.com (with Berkeley EERC photos) says the distinctive structural feature is a truss system above the first floor. Redwood Park (half acre) lies next to it.
- Built 1969-1972; architects William Pereira and Harry Som.
