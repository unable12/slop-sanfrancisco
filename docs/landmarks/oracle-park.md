# Oracle Park

## Facts
- Opened March 31, 2000 (as Pacific Bell Park); home of the SF Giants. Coordinates 37 46 43 N, 122 23 21 W (Wikipedia). OSM relation 7325085 (height 45 m) at 37.778646,-122.38958; OSM ways 46274021, 780006768 tagged as pier.
- Capacity: 41,331 (2021-present, Wikipedia infobox); SABR page says 42,300 as of 2025. Sources disagree.
- Field dimensions (Wikipedia infobox, after the 2019-20 bullpen move): left field line 339 ft (103 m), left field 354 ft (108 m), left-center 399 ft (122 m), center 391 ft (119 m), right-center 415 ft (126 m), right field 365 ft (111 m), right field line 309 ft (94 m), backstop 48 ft (15 m).
- Fence heights: left field 8 ft, center 7 ft, dead center 10 ft, right-center 20 ft, right field 24 ft (7.3 m) brick wall (Wikipedia says it honours Willie Mays).
- McCovey Cove: the part of China Basin (SF Bay) beyond the right-field wall; a public promenade runs between the wall and the water.
- Clock towers: two main clock towers each 122 ft tall, plus two shorter ones (47 ft) on the bay side (SABR page). The main entrance is the Willie Mays Gate behind home plate.
- Coca-Cola bottle: 80 ft (24 m) long contoured bottle with slides, behind the left-field bleachers (Wikipedia). Giant four-fingered baseball glove: 26 ft high x 32 ft wide, made of steel and fibreglass, more than 500 ft from home plate (SABR).
- Light towers: UNKNOWN (not found).
- Exterior: brick facade with brick patterns echoing baseball seams (SABR); the stadium is oriented so the park looks across the bay (Wikipedia notes it was designed 15 degrees clockwise from its final angle in the 1996 plan).

- OSM footprint of stadium relation 7325085, minimum bounding rectangle: 212.3 x 191.2 m (696 x 627 ft), rotated about 45 degrees.
