# Mission Dolores (Mission San Francisco de Asis) and Basilica

## Facts
- Adobe chapel: construction began 1788 (36,000 adobe bricks), walls done 1790, church finished 1791; oldest intact structure in San Francisco. Walls four feet (1.2 m) thick, redwood roof beams, tile roof and floors. Dimensions: church 114 ft long x 22 ft wide (California missions fact card, factcards.califa.org). No bell tower on the old chapel; a balcony crosses the front on four Corinthian-style columns (same fact card). Coordinates 37 45 51.8 N, 122 25 37.3 W (Wikipedia).
- Mural behind the altar: 22 x 20 ft (Wikipedia).
- Basilica next door: built 1913-1918 (Mission Dolores Church; churrigueresque ornamentation added in 1926; architect Willis Polk restored the adobe chapel); minor basilica since 1952. OSM way 256442760 at 37.764343,-122.426951: height 14 m, start_date 1918 (OSM height is the main roof, not the towers). Tower height: UNKNOWN in opened sources.
- A Gothic Revival brick church from 1876 was destroyed in 1906; the 1918 basilica replaced it.

- OSM: Old Mission Dolores way 256442765 (start_date 1791, height 8 m, ele 23) at 37.764185,-122.426812; Basilica way 256442760 (height 14 m, 1918); Mission Dolores Cemetery way 1421390128 (start 1830).

- OSM footprint of the Basilica (way 256442760): minimum bounding rectangle 58.6 x 33.1 m (192 x 109 ft).
