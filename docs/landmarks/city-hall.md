# San Francisco City Hall

## Facts
- Coordinates 37.77919 N, 122.41914 W (Wikipedia). OSM relation 7261820: height=30, building:colour #e3e3de (OSM height is the main roof, not the dome).
- Architects Bakewell & Brown (Arthur Brown Jr.); construction 1913-1915, opened Dec 28, 1915 (Wikipedia: Mayor moved in then; scaffolding removed July 1916).
- Dome: 307.5 ft (93.7 m) above the Civic Center (Wikipedia); a search snippet gave 307 ft and PCAD 306 ft. Dome diameter 112 ft (34 m) (Wikipedia, archiseek). Dome resting on 4 x 50-ton and 4 x 20-ton girders.
- Capitol comparison disagrees: Wikipedia lead says the dome is 42 ft taller than the US Capitol dome, the Architecture section says 19 ft, archiseek says 11 ft 7 in.
- Footprint: two sources disagree. Wikipedia: 390 ft (120 m) between Van Ness Ave and Polk St by 273 ft (83 m) between Grove and McAllister Sts; occupies two city blocks, over 500,000 sq ft of floor area. Archiseek: plan 408 x 285 ft.
- Storeys: Wikipedia infobox "5, including ground floor"; Time Out mentions a five-storey colonnade around the rotunda.
- Dome colour: gilded in part; a CBS report says the dome carries $400,000 of gold leaf (after the 1990s restoration). Which parts (ribs, lantern) are gilded: UNKNOWN. Archiseek mentions blue and gold burnished ironwork.
- Facade order: UNKNOWN. A Doric colonnade is NOT confirmed by any opened source (Doric columns turned up for Arthur Brown's Federal Building and the Opera House, not City Hall). Facade is gray Madera County California granite (Wikipedia, archiseek), French Renaissance / Beaux-Arts, dome modeled on Les Invalides (Mansart).
- 1989 Loma Prieta quake twisted the dome 4 in on its base; base isolation completed 1999.

- OSM footprint (relation 7261820) minimum bounding rectangle: 126.6 x 97.8 m (415 x 321 ft), about 12,400 m2. This is bigger than Wikipedia's 390 x 273 ft (119 x 83 m), likely because OSM includes the wings/pavilions; archiseek gives 408 x 285 ft (124 x 87 m).
