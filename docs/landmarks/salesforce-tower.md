# Salesforce Tower

## Facts
- 415 Mission St. Coordinates 37.7899 N, 122.3969 W (Wikipedia); OSM way 431972186 at 37.789775,-122.396914.
- Height: 1,070 ft (326 m) to the top of the crown; roof height 970 ft (296 m) (Wikipedia). OSM height=326, building:levels=61.
- Floors: 61. Completed/opened Jan 2018. Designed by Cesar Pelli (Pelli Clarke Pelli).
- Plan: "obelisk-shaped"; a search-result summary (Architect Magazine / Dezeen family of pages, not opened) describes it as square in plan with rounded corners, with the top third tapering softly. Wikipedia does not state the plan shape.
- Base width: UNKNOWN from text. Land lot 50,514 sq ft (4,692.9 m2) with 75.21 ft frontage (not building width). OSM footprint minimum rectangle: 54.4 x 54.2 m (178 x 178 ft), rotated about 40 degrees from the grid axis.
- Taper: silhouette tapers smoothly toward the top; no ratio given.
- Crown: the top 150 ft (46 m) above the 61st floor is largely ornamental (Wikipedia). Open lattice height: UNKNOWN (no source mentions a lattice; a search summary said the walls continue into a transparent crown). LED installation "Day for Night" by Jim Campbell: a nine-story light sculpture with about 11,000 inward-facing LEDs on the top six floors, visible from up to 30 mi (50 km), activated May 21, 2018.
- Facade: glass and steel curtain wall on a steel frame around a reinforced concrete core; lattice of white aluminium fins and perforated sunshades projecting up to 2 ft (0.6 m) beyond the glass.
- Foundation: 42 piles about 300 ft (91 m) deep, 14 ft thick mat.
