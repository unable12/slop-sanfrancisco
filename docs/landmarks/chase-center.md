# Chase Center

## Facts
- Opened Sept 6, 2019 (Metallica + SF Symphony). Address Third St and 16th St, Mission Bay. Coordinates 37 46 05 N, 122 23 15 W (Wikipedia); OSM way 579646390 at 37.767888,-122.387421 with height=38.1 m (indoor arena, leisure=stadium).
- Capacity: basketball 18,064; concerts 19,500 (Wikipedia). 11-12 acre site (search: 11-acre; Wikipedia: 12-acre site purchased).
- Facade: rainscreen of white aluminium panels, 5,500 panels in 14 different shapes in about 950 "mega panels", curving and sloping (Architect's Newspaper, via search summary; page not opened separately). Glass is mainly in the atrium and gatehouse (40,000 sq ft curtain wall).
- Height and diameter: UNKNOWN in text (OSM 38.1 m height). Massing is a stacked "drum"; no oval dimensions found.
- Two office buildings (about 180 ft tall, 13 floors) on the same site per a search summary.

- OSM footprint (way 579646390) minimum bounding rectangle: 155.1 x 153.5 m (509 x 504 ft), almost square, so a near-circular drum of about 155 m width with a 38.1 m OSM height.
