# Palace of Fine Arts

## Facts
- Coordinates 37 48 10 N, 122 26 54 W (Wikipedia); OSM way 288371295 at 37.802918,-122.448385 (height 48 m, start_date 1915).
- Built 1915 for the Panama-Pacific International Exposition (Bernard Maybeck); demolished 1964 and rebuilt in poured-in-place concrete and steel, 1964-1974.
- Rotunda: 162 ft (49 m) high, open; octagonal with eight piers; hemispherical dome with eight coffered insets. Diameter UNKNOWN. A search summary mentions another source giving 135 ft; Wikipedia and a state historic survey give 162 ft.
- Colonnade: a pergola/colonnade about 1,100 ft (340 m) wide around the rotunda and lagoon (Wikipedia: "wide, 1,100 ft pergola around a central rotunda"). Column height UNKNOWN. A search summary mentions Corinthian columns and "30" (not verified).
- Lagoon: small artificial lagoon in front (east side of the rotunda); reflects the buildings.
- Exhibition hall: large curved (semicircular) building behind the colonnades, separated from the lagoon by them; now houses the Exploratorium (1969-2013) and the 966-seat Palace of Fine Arts Theatre. Dimensions UNKNOWN.
- Site: about 17 acres (search summary).

- OSM details: main way 288371295 (building=temple, height 48 m). Rotunda parts: dome part way 456820271 (height 51 m, min_height 25 m, dome roof 2 m) centred 37.802918,-122.448404; drum part 456820274 (40 m, min 25 m); part 1549377350 (35 m, min 25 m); seven 'column' parts of height 36 m (ways 1549377328, 1549377331, 1549377334, 1549377335, 1549377338, 1549377341, 1549377345) plus a 36 m part 1549377344, arranged around the rotunda and the colonnade ends (so colonnade/rotunda columns about 36 m tall in OSM; this looks too tall for plain colonnade columns and may be the rotunda piers); lagoon-side colonnade/pergola parts 17 m tall (ways 1550664397, 1550664399, 1550664401) and 12 m (ways 1550664405-1550664409); exhibition hall roofs 'building=roof' 19-21 m (ways 288371306, 288371310, 288371313, 288371314); small pavilions 1-5 m.
