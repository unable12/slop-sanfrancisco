# Coit Tower

## Facts
- Height: 210 ft (64 m) (Wikipedia). OSM way 28824850: height=64, ele=89, building:colour #E0E0E0, material concrete, at 37.802377,-122.405834.
- Built 1932-1933, Art Deco, unpainted reinforced concrete; architects Arthur Brown Jr. and Henry Temple Howard.
- Structure: three nested concrete cylinders. Outermost is a tapering fluted shaft 180 ft (55 m) tall that carries the viewing platform; middle shaft holds the stairs; inner shaft holds the lift.
- Observation deck is 32 ft (9.8 m) below the top, with an arcade (arched openings) and skylights above it.
- Diameter: UNKNOWN (not found in any opened source).
- Base: a rotunda at the base houses display space and a gift shop.
- Ground elevation: Telegraph Hill listed at 285 ft (87 m) in a search summary (page: Wikipedia Telegraph Hill); the tower base elevation is not stated. OSM ele=89 m at the tower footprint. Tower is in Pioneer Park.
- Coordinates: 37 48 09 N, 122 24 21 W (Wikipedia).

- OSM footprint (way 28824850, includes the base rotunda): minimum bounding rectangle 22.3 x 22.1 m (73 x 73 ft), about 492 m2, so the tower plus base block is roughly 22 m across; shaft diameter is smaller.
