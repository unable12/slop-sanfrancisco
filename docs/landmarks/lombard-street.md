# Lombard Street (crooked block)

## Facts
- The crooked block on Russian Hill runs between Hyde and Leavenworth Streets; one-way downhill, eight sharp turns (hairpins), red brick paving. Sign at top recommends 5 mph.
- Length: about 600 ft (180 m) along the road, 412.5 ft (125.7 m) as a straight line (Wikipedia).
- Grade: the hill's natural grade is 27 percent; the street was built in 1922 (design suggested by property owner Carl Henry) to reduce it. Resulting grade of the street itself: 16 percent per OSM (see below); Wikipedia does not give it.
- Flower beds and stairs: Wikipedia text does not mention them; search snippets mention hydrangeas and side stairs for pedestrians. Not verified from an opened page.
- Powell-Hyde cable car stops at the top of the block on Hyde Street.

- OSM way 402111597 (the crooked block, centre 37.8021078,-122.4188085): highway=residential, oneway, lanes=1, incline=-16%, surface=bricks, maxspeed 25 mph with advisory 5 mph, sidewalk=separate, foot=no on the roadway itself. So OSM gives a 16 percent grade for the street surface, which agrees with the komoot snippet (the 27 percent figure is the hill's natural grade). No footprint or height data (road only).
