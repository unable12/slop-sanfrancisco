# Cable car turntable (Powell & Market)

## Facts
- Location: Powell St at Market St (Hallidie Plaza area), terminus of the Powell-Mason and Powell-Hyde lines. OSM node 8641952045 "Cable Car Turntable (Powell & Market)" at 37.784711,-122.407699; unnamed OSM way 197974389 at 37.784712,-122.407698 is the turntable outline (assumed; no tags).
- Diameter: UNKNOWN in text sources. The OSM outline fits a 5.1 x 5.1 m (17 x 17 ft) bounding box, which is the best available figure; treat as approximate (not a sourced dimension).
- Operation: cable cars cannot reverse; the crew gets off, grips the chassis and pushes the car round by hand on a revolving wooden platform (Lonely Planet). The 1984 turntable (10 tons, made in Oakland) was hard to turn, so pipe handles were added; the current turntable was installed Oct-Nov 2002 with pipe handles and redwood facing boards (cable-car-guy.com).
- Other cable car turntables (OSM): Hyde & Beach 37.807041,-122.421237; Taylor & Bay 37.805232,-122.415236.
