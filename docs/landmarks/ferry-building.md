# Ferry Building

## Facts
- Location 37.7955 N, 122.3937 W (Wikipedia); OSM way 558731934 at 37.795538,-122.39343 (relation 7943818 has the name only).
- Designed 1892 by A. Page Brown, Beaux-Arts; opened July 13, 1898. Wikipedia: restored 2002-2003.
- Clock tower: 245 ft (75 m) tall, four clock dials each 22 ft (6.7 m) in diameter (clock by E. Howard, 1898; weight shaft 48 ft, pendulum 16 ft).
- Main building length: not stated as a whole in Wikipedia; OSM footprint (below) gives 201 m (660 ft) long, matching the 660 ft nave. The Great Nave (second floor, runs the length of the building) is 660 ft (200 m) long; 22 arches per side along the nave (44 arches total).
- Storeys: 2 original floors; a third floor added in 1947/1950 was removed in the 2002 restoration, which restored the nave to its two-story height. Wikipedia still says the "second and third floors" hold offices, so treat as ground + upper floor(s), roughly 2 to 3 levels. Not stated explicitly as a single number.
- Arcade: the whole base on both frontages is an arched arcade.
- Tower inspiration: possibly the Giralda in Seville (Wikipedia says no evidence).
- Material: reinforced building (survived 1906 and 1989 quakes); brick and terra cotta arches in the nave; marble and mosaic floor in the nave.

- OSM footprint (way 558731934, building=terminal, height=15 m, ele 1): minimum bounding rectangle 201.0 x 56.1 m (660 x 184 ft), about 11,300 m2, long axis rotated about 126 degrees (i.e. parallel to the Embarcadero). Tower height not tagged separately (the 15 m is the main roof/eave).
