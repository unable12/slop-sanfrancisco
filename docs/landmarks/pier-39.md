# Pier 39

## Facts
- Opened Oct 4, 1978 (developer Warren Simmons). Coordinates 37.8100 N, 122.4104 W (Wikipedia). OSM pier ways 199829618, 28518695.
- Length: UNKNOWN. Complex described as a 45-acre waterfront complex with a 5-acre park and a 300-berth marina (PIER 39 press release seen in a search summary, not opened).
- Sea lions: first hauled out on the K-Dock in the marina after the Oct 1989 Loma Prieta quake (PIER 39 official page). Wikipedia says they are on docks in the marina. A news source in a search result puts the haul-out on the west side of the pier. Record counts: 1,701 in Nov 2009 (older record), over 2,100 in 2024 per PIER 39 (search summary).
- Carousel: a two-story carousel, one of the pier's dominant features, near the end of the pier (Wikipedia). Diameter/height UNKNOWN.
- Two-level open-air marketplace of over 100 shops/restaurants (search summary).

- OSM pier way 199829618 (man_made=pier, tourism=attraction): minimum bounding rectangle 355.7 x 134.3 m (1,167 x 441 ft), about 47,800 m2. This is an outline measured from OSM, not a published pier length.

- OSM: no building height found on the pier ways or the Pier 39 relation 7943822.
