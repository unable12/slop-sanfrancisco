# de Young Museum (Herzog & de Meuron building)

## Facts
- Current building opened Oct 15, 2005; architects Herzog & de Meuron with Fong & Chan. OSM relation 1652482 at 37.771498,-122.46872: building=museum, height=13 m.
- Hamon Observation Tower: 144 ft (44 m) tall (Wikipedia), tallest point in Golden Gate Park; 9th floor viewing level reached by elevator; tower is made of copper. OSM has no separate height seen for the tower.
- Twist: UNKNOWN as a number. A review in The Plan (via search summary) says the tower is not symmetrical and "twists up, vortex-like" to a viewing terrace.
- Facade: entire exterior clad in 163,118 sq ft (15,154 m2) of copper plates, perforated and dimpled to echo light through tree canopy; expected to oxidise to green. Shapes are cut into the top to open gardens/courtyards; fabricated by Zahner.
- Footprint: UNKNOWN in text (Plan review mentions a length of about 420 ft, via search summary). OSM footprint dims added below if available.
- Seismic: can move up to 3 ft on sliding plates and dampers.

- OSM tower: Hamon Tower way 444230154 (height 10 m, tourism=viewpoint) at 37.772036,-122.468327 plus stacked 5 m building:part slices: 13-18, 18-23, 23-28, 28-33, 33-38, 38-43, 43-46, 46-51 m (ways 1418750068-1418750073, 1418972816, 1418972817). The slice centres drift from (37.772038,-122.468327) at the bottom to (37.772059,-122.468297) at the top, about 3 m shift: this suggests a lean or twist in the mapped tower. Top at 51 m is higher than Wikipedia's 144 ft (43.9 m), so the OSM slices probably include the museum roof level or are approximate.

- OSM footprint (relation 1652482) minimum bounding rectangle: 153.8 x 76.1 m (504 x 250 ft), about 11,700 m2, rotated about 42 degrees.
