# Salesforce Park (Transit Center rooftop park)

## Facts
- Rooftop park on the Salesforce Transit Center, opened Aug 12, 2018; 5.4 acres; designed by PWP Landscape Architecture. Coordinates of the Transit Center 37.7897 N, 122.3966 W (Wikipedia). OSM: relation 8524888 (leisure=park) at 37.789117,-122.396871; transit center way 542375290 (building:levels 5, height 30 m).
- Length: runs the full length of the Transit Center, "nearly four blocks". The Transit Center is about 1,430 ft (440 m) long and 165 ft (50 m) wide (Wikipedia), which also bounds the park footprint. Park width: UNKNOWN as a separate figure; with the 165 ft building width the park is at most that wide.
- Height: the park is 70 ft above the Grand Hall (TJPA). Height above the street: UNKNOWN in opened sources (OSM building height 30 m for the whole centre).
- Contents: 600 trees, 16,000 plants, 13 botanical areas, an amphitheater, a restaurant, a fountain art piece of 247 water jets over 1,200 ft; a free 20-passenger gondola from Mission/Fremont to the park.

- OSM footprint of the Transit Center (way 542375290): minimum bounding rectangle 436.6 x 55.3 m (1,432 x 181 ft), matching Wikipedia's 1,430 ft length; building height 30 m.
