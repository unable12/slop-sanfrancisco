# Grace Cathedral

## Facts
- Address 1100 California St, Nob Hill; OSM way 32946942 at 37.791833,-122.413497 (height 53 m, ele 89 m).
- French Gothic (Wikipedia infobox); designed by George Frederick Bodley, completed by Lewis P. Hobart; ferroconcrete; cornerstone 1910, built in four campaigns 1928-1964; opened for services 1934; completed 1964.
- Length 329 ft (100 m); width at the transepts 162 ft (49 m); central spire (flèche) 247 ft (75 m) above street level; towers 174 ft (53 m) above street level; entry floor 20 ft (6.1 m) above street level; sanctuary floor 299.5 ft (91.3 m) above sea level (Wikipedia). Under the earlier Bodley plan it would have been 275 x 180 ft.
- Plan: cruciform with nave and transepts (stated implicitly: width "at the transepts"); two bell towers (one finished first; carillon in the towers).
- Rose window: UNKNOWN (Wikipedia text mentions stained glass, 7,290 sq ft total, but no rose window detail).

- OSM footprint (way 32946942) minimum bounding rectangle: 95.7 x 43.4 m (314 x 142 ft); height tag 53 m (the tower height; matches Wikipedia 174 ft = 53 m).
